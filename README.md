# Durga Khasta Corner — Website

A one-page website for Durga Khasta Corner, a legendary breakfast shop on Latouche Road, Lucknow, serving crisp Khasta and Bada with white peas, spicy potatoes, onion and green chilli for over 80 years.

## What the site is

A single-page, mobile-friendly website (a single static `index.html`, Tailwind via CDN) with:

- Hero section — the shop as "the khasta Lucknow wakes up for", with its 80+ years, daily 4:00 AM – 3:00 PM hours and approximate cost for two
- About section — the shop's story, its counter service and traditional leaf-plate serving, plus an Opening Hours card
- Menu Highlights — 6 cards: Signature Khasta, Bada, Khasta Matar, Spicy Aloo Sabzi, Jalebi and the Patta Plate, with no prices listed (visitors are asked to check at the counter for today's prices)
- Visit Us section — address, phone, hours, call and Google Maps directions links
- Enquiry form that opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message (for bulk orders, timings or anything else)

All copy is in clean professional English.

## Visitor notes

- A small, no-frills counter shop: there are no tables — patrons gather at the counter and plates are handed over fresh, served the traditional way on a dried leaf.
- Mornings are the busiest time; visitors are advised to come early and allow a little extra time during peak hours.
