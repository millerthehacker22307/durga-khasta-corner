About Durga Khasta Corner

Located at 91/19, Latouche Road in Ghasyari Mandi, Naka Hindola, Durga Khasta Corner is a small, no-frills shop with a very big following. There are no tables and no fuss here — patrons gather at the counter, and hot khasta and bada are handed over fresh, served the traditional way on a dried leaf.

The shop has been a morning ritual for generations of Lucknow families for more than eight decades. Come early: the khastas are at their best straight out of the kadhai, and the morning crowd knows it.
